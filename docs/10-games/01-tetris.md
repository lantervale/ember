# Tetris

![Tetris: a board of falling pieces beside its score, next piece, lines and level](tetris.png "ember · tetris")

Line clears flash and spark.

| Does | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Move | Left, Right | cross or left stick, left and right |
| Fall faster | Down | cross or left stick, down |
| Drop | Space | cross, up |
| Turn | Up | A (south) |
| New game | N | View |
| Menu: new game, leaderboard, music and sound | Escape | Menu |

[Play in your browser](https://lantervale.github.io/ember/live/index.html?wasm=games-tetris.wasm.gz) · [The code](https://github.com/lantervale/ember/tree/main/games/tetris) · `go run ./games/tetris`
