# Flap

![Flap: a pixel-art bird between green pipes under a cloudy sky, beside its score and best](flap.png "ember · flap")

The clouds scroll slower than the pipes, and the physics steps at 120 Hz whatever the frame rate. Pixel art by Megacrash (CC0).

| Does | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Flap | Space, Up, W or a click | A (south) |
| New game | N | View |
| Menu: new game, leaderboard, music and sound | Escape | Menu |

[Play in your browser](https://lantervale.github.io/ember/live/index.html?wasm=games-flap.wasm.gz) · [The code](https://github.com/lantervale/ember/tree/main/games/flap) · `go run ./games/flap`
