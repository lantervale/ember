# 2048

![2048: a board of numbered tiles beside its score and best tile](2048.png "ember · 2048")

Two equal tiles merge into their sum, and merges pop. Reach 2048, and keep going until the board is full.

| Does | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Slide | arrows | cross or left stick |
| New game | N | View |
| Menu: new game, leaderboard, music and sound | Escape | Menu |

[Play in your browser](https://lantervale.github.io/ember/live/index.html?wasm=games-2048.wasm.gz) · [The code](https://github.com/lantervale/ember/tree/main/games/2048) · `go run ./games/2048`
