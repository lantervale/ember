# Snake

![Snake: a green snake chasing an apple on a checkered board beside its score and length](snake.png "ember · snake")

The snake glides between cells and every apple bursts. It waits for your first move.

| Does | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Steer | arrows or WASD | cross or left stick |
| New game | N | View |
| Menu: new game, leaderboard, music and sound | Escape | Menu |

[Play in your browser](https://lantervale.github.io/ember/live/index.html?wasm=games-snake.wasm.gz) · [The code](https://github.com/lantervale/ember/tree/main/games/snake) · `go run ./games/snake`
