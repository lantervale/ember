# 0164. Indic, Khmer, Myanmar and USE scripts are shaped by HarfBuzz's syllabic shapers

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-23
- Area: rendering
- Owner: engine

## Context

[0157](0157-arabic-is-joined-and-marks-attached-by-harfbuzzs-stages-and-masks.md) shaped the joining scripts; Devanagari and the other Brahmic scripts were drawn in their logical order, a pre-base matra after its consonant, no reph, half or below form, and a mark a face's GPOS does not place, or a precomposed character it lacks, was left as it was.

## Decision

- `internal/shape` ports HarfBuzz 14.2.1's Indic shaper (the nine Indic scripts, their second and third version tags, the latter routed to the USE), its Khmer and Myanmar shapers and the Universal Shaping Engine for the scripts HarfBuzz gives it, chosen by the script tag the face's GSUB has as HarfBuzz chooses. Each finds syllables with HarfBuzz's Ragel grammars, compiled once to Go regular expressions and matched longest first; inserts dotted circles in broken clusters and before forbidden vowel sequences; reorders as HarfBuzz does (base, reph, pre-base matras and consonants, `pref`), and runs its features in its stages with its masks and pause functions.
- Plans carry HarfBuzz's map flags: a feature per syllable matches only within its syllable, a manual joiner is matched rather than passed over, and default ignorable characters are skipped as HarfBuzz's skipping iterator skips them, then drawn as the face's space. Glyphs carry what substitutions did to them (substituted, ligated, multiplied), as the reorderings read it.
- The category tables come from Unicode 17's `IndicSyllabicCategory.txt`, `IndicPositionalCategory.txt` and the files `gen-use-table.py` reads, with HarfBuzz 14.2.1's `ms-use` overrides and vowel constraints, generated by `go generate` as HarfBuzz's own generators map them.
- Text is normalised first as HarfBuzz does: characters a face lacks (and every character of a syllabic script) decomposed into those it has, marks sorted by HarfBuzz's modified combining classes, then composed again where the face has the result. A face with no GPOS has its marks placed by their combining classes from the glyphs' boxes (HarfBuzz's fallback), under the default and Arabic shapers.
- draw and ui shape each run of one script apart, and draw keeps a cluster's joiners a face lacks so they still act on its shaping.

## Alternatives

- go-text/typesetting's port of HarfBuzz: complete, but go.mod is frozen. Ragel's generated state tables: unreadable and a build tool; regular expressions keep the grammars as HarfBuzz writes them.
- HarfBuzz's main branch rather than 14.2.1: the hb-shape the tests compare against is 14.2.1, so its data and its few differing rules (Khmer's coeng count, the USE's joining of generic bases) are kept.

## Consequences

The Indic, Khmer, Myanmar and USE texts of the Mac's faces and of HarfBuzz's own in-house tests shape as `hb-shape` does, and ui's Devanagari lays out and draws as Chrome's. The joining scripts HarfBuzz now gives the USE (Mongolian, N'Ko, Adlam…) keep the Arabic shaper's stages, and the Thai, Lao and Hangul shapers are still left out.
